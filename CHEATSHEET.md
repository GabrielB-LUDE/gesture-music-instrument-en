# Gesture Music Instrument - Cheatsheet

*Hands. Beats. Creativity.*

> **Important:** when playing, the **pose** of the hand counts - not its position.

English text version of `Cheatsheet_gesture.png` (the image itself is in German).

## 1. Grab a beat
1. With your left hand, pick a beat in the library on the left.
2. Pinch: bring thumb and index finger together and hold briefly. The beat is grabbed.
3. Move the hand back into the free area. Now you control the sound.

## 2. Control the sound (one hand)
**Fingers = density**

| 1 finger | 2 fingers | 3 fingers | 4 fingers | Fist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| very sparse | medium density | denser | maximum density | mute |

- **Tilt = volume:** tilt the hand outwards → louder, inwards → quieter.
- **Thumb = timbre:** spread the thumb → more open / brighter.
- **Pinch = grab:** thumb + index finger → pinch / grab.

## 3. Hold the sound / freeze
1. Set up the sound first.
2. Turn the wrist over: back of the hand to the camera.
3. The values are frozen.

The loop keeps running, but fingers, volume and timbre no longer change it.
**Release again:** turn the wrist back → palm towards the camera.

## 4. Fuse two loops
1. Grab two loops and hold / freeze both.
2. Bring the hands together.
3. Hold them close together briefly.

The two beats are combined into one loop. Afterwards the hand tilt becomes the shared volume control.

## 5. Place a loop (on the rack)
1. Pinch with the loop in your hand.
2. Hold the pinch and open again.
3. The loop flies to the rack on the right.

It keeps playing there. The hand is free again.

## 6. Get a loop back from the rack
1. Empty hand to the rack on the right, select the slot.
2. Pinch.
3. The loop comes back into the hand.

## 7. Delete a loop
- **Option 1 - long pinch**
  - At the rack: hold the pinch → the progress bar fills → the loop is deleted.
  - With a loop in the hand: hold the pinch → the loop is thrown away.
- **Option 2 - trash bin:** carry the loop in your hand to the trash bin and hold it there briefly → the loop is deleted.

## 8. Riser & drop
- **Riser - all hands open wide:** all hands open wide. Ideally 4 fingers extended, thumb spread → shared build-up / riser.
- **Drop - everyone makes a fist:** after a built-up riser, everyone makes a fist together → DROP! The fist mutes the individual loops briefly - this triggers the drop.

## 9. Live flow (quick overview)
1. **Grab** - library → pinch
2. **Shape** - fingers = density, tilt = volume, thumb = timbre
3. **Freeze** - turn the wrist → hold values
4. **Fuse** - grab a second loop → bring both hands together
5. **Fuse** - short pinch → rack
6. **Park** / **Build** - open hands → riser
7. **Drop** - fist → drop

## The 5 most important gestures
Fingers = density · Tilt = volume · Thumb = timbre · Turn = hold · Pinch = grab

## Important tips
- Extend or curl your fingers clearly.
- Half-open fingers can sit between two states.
- When freezing, turn the whole wrist, don't just tilt the hand slightly.
- Hand position does not matter for sound shaping (only for library, rack and trash bin).

## Simple rule of thumb
**OPEN** = more · **LEAN** = volume · **THUMB** = tone · **TURN** = hold · **PINCH** = grab

*Make music with your hands.*
